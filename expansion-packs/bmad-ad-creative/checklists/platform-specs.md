# Platform Specifications Checklist

## Meta (Facebook & Instagram)

### Image Ads
- [ ] **File Type:** JPG or PNG
- [ ] **Ratio:** 1.91:1 to 1:1
- [ ] **Resolution:** At least 1080 x 1080 pixels
- [ ] **Text in Image:** Image contains little to no overlay text.

### Video Ads
- [ ] **File Type:** MP4, MOV or GIF
- [ ] **Ratio:** 1:1 (for feeds) and 9:16 (for stories)
- [ ] **Resolution:** At least 1080 x 1080 pixels
- [ ] **Video Duration:** 1 second to 241 minutes
- [ ] **Captions:** Video captions are recommended.

## Google Ads

### Responsive Display Ads
- [ ] **Images (up to 15):**
  - **Landscape (1.91:1):** 1200 x 628 (min required: 600 x 314)
  - **Square (1:1):** 1200 x 1200 (min required: 300 x 300)
- [ ] **Logos (up to 5):**
  - **Landscape (4:1):** 1200 x 300 (min required: 512 x 128)
  - **Square (1:1):** 1200 x 1200 (min required: 128 x 128)
- [ ] **Videos (up to 5):**
  - **YouTube link:** Any ratio, recommended > 30 seconds.

### App Campaigns
- [ ] **Images (up to 20):** Various sizes, JPG, GIF, PNG, max 5MB.
- [ ] **Videos (up to 20):** YouTube link, no duration limit, landscape, portrait, or square.
- [ ] **HTML5 (up to 20):** Zipped, max 1MB, and no more than 40 files.
