# Creative Quality Checklist

## Copy
- [ ] **Clarity:** Is the message instantly understandable?
- [ ] **Conciseness:** Is the copy as short as it can be without losing impact?
- [ ] **Benefit-Oriented:** Does the copy focus on the benefits for the customer, not just the features?
- [ ] **Strong Hook:** Does the headline or first line grab attention?
- [ ] **Clear CTA:** Is the call to action clear, compelling, and easy to follow?
- [ ] **Grammar & Spelling:** Is the copy free of any grammatical errors or typos?

## Visuals (Image & Video)
- [ ] **Stopping Power:** Is the visual striking enough to stop someone from scrolling?
- [ ] **High Quality:** Is the image/video high-resolution and professionally produced?
- [ ] **Relevance:** Does the visual directly relate to the product or message?
- [ ] **Focal Point:** Is there a clear focal point that draws the viewer's eye?
- [ ] **Branding:** Is the branding present but not overly intrusive?
- [ ] **Mobile First:** Is the creative optimized for viewing on a mobile device (e.g., readable text, vertical video for stories)?
- [ ] **Video Pacing:** (For video) Is the pacing effective at holding attention? Are the first 3 seconds impactful?
